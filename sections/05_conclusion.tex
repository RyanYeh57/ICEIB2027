\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
GarbageGo is a proposed course project that helps residents choose a waste collection point by comparing a truck's expected arrival with a pedestrian journey and a safety buffer. The proposed division of work connects open data, spatial routing, arrival estimation, recommendation, and a natural-language interface.

The existing data audit provides a practical starting point. It also shows why vehicle timestamps, approximate geocoding, and current vehicle-to-stop relationships require attention. A schedule-based demonstration and clearly identified uncertainty can establish the workflow before further arrival-time methods are evaluated.
